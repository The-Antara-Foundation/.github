## 🌍 About The Antara Foundation (TAF)

In remote villages across India, reaching a health center can take hours—a delay that can cost lives. **The Antara Foundation** partners with governments and local communities to support the public health system in delivering scalable, sustainable solutions for maternal and child health.

Our core focus areas include:

* **Frontline Enablement:** Building the capabilities of over a million Community Health Workers (CHWs) and ASHAs to improve service delivery at the grassroots level.
* **Risk Prioritization:** Utilizing data-driven insights to identify and manage high-risk pregnancies and neonatal cases efficiently.
* **Last-Mile Intervention:** Creating ecosystems of care, such as *Poshan Clinics*, to combat malnutrition and ensure high-quality healthcare reaches the most marginalized communities.

---

## 💻 The Digital Public Health Program (DPHP)

The DPHP team builds the technological backbone that translates TAF’s mission into actionable, scalable realities. We engineer high-performance platforms that allow health workers, program officers, and policymakers to log data, track high-risk cases in real-time, and visualize demographic and geospatial health trends.

Our engineering philosophy focuses on crafting robust, maintainable systems using clean architectures to ensure our codebases remain adaptable to the rapidly evolving needs of on-ground health infrastructure and complex external integrations.
